Editorial & Fact-Checking Policy

Our commitment to factual accuracy, independent hardware testing, reproducible code, and transparent disclosures on Ahadex Blog (ahadex.online).

1. Peer Review & Code Execution Standards

Before any engineering tutorial or benchmark review is cleared for publication on Ahadex Blog:

  • Every shell command and code snippet is independently cloned and executed in a fresh cleanroom container (Docker/Podman) by founder Mohammad Ahad or our technical reviewers.
  • Performance claims must include explicit hardware specifications, kernel versions, and ambient test conditions.
  • We avoid unverified vendor hype and marketing claims.

2. Use of Artificial Intelligence Tools

While we cover artificial intelligence as a primary beat, all Ahadex Blog articles are authored, synthesized, and verified by human technical experts. We use automated tools for spelling checks and static analysis, but never publish synthetic, unverified AI text.

3. Corrections Policy

We promptly correct technical inaccuracies. If an error is identified, we append a timestamped correction note at the base of the article explaining the modification. Readers can submit corrections directly via WhatsApp (+971 50 797 5837) or our Contact Form.
